# Robot Lab Options

## Academic Laboratory Setup

### Core Infrastructure Requirements

#### Physical Space Considerations
- **Minimum Area**: 100 square meters (1,000 sq ft) for basic humanoid robotics lab
- **Ceiling Height**: At least 3 meters (10 feet) to accommodate full-size humanoid robots
- **Flooring**: Non-slip, durable surface capable of supporting robot weight (up to 100kg+)
- **Power Outlets**: Multiple grounded outlets with circuit protection for equipment safety
- **Ventilation**: Adequate air circulation for heat dissipation from computing equipment

#### Network Infrastructure
- **Bandwidth**: Enterprise-grade internet (100 Mbps minimum, 1 Gbps recommended)
- **Wireless**: 802.11ac or newer with multiple access points for coverage
- **Wired**: Gigabit Ethernet backbone with PoE+ for cameras and sensors
- **Network Segmentation**: Separate VLANs for robot control, data, and guest access

### Essential Equipment Categories

#### Computing Resources
- **Workstations**: 5-10 high-performance workstations for development
- **Servers**: Dedicated servers for simulation, data processing, and AI training
- **Edge Devices**: On-board computers for robot platforms (NVIDIA Jetson, Raspberry Pi)
- **Storage**: Network-attached storage (NAS) with 10+ TB capacity for data logging

#### Robot Platforms
- **Humanoid Robots**: At least 2-3 humanoid platforms (e.g., NAO, Pepper, or custom builds)
- **Mobile Base Platforms**: Wheeled or tracked platforms for foundational work
- **Manipulator Arms**: Robotic arms for manipulation research
- **Simulation Hardware**: High-end GPUs for physics simulation

#### Tools and Instruments
- **Basic Tools**: Screwdrivers, wrenches, cable strippers, multimeters
- **Advanced Tools**: Oscilloscopes, signal generators, thermal imaging cameras
- **Assembly Tools**: 3D printer, laser cutter (if available), workbenches
- **Safety Equipment**: Emergency stops, safety barriers, first aid kit

## Commercial Laboratory Setup

### Industrial-Grade Considerations
- **Robustness**: Equipment designed for 24/7 operation
- **Scalability**: Infrastructure that can expand with program growth
- **Security**: Access controls, surveillance, and data protection
- **Compliance**: Meeting industry standards and certifications

### Enterprise Software Stack
- **Simulation Environments**: Gazebo, NVIDIA Isaac Sim, Unity Robotics
- **Development Tools**: ROS/ROS2 with enterprise support
- **Project Management**: GitLab, Jira, or similar platforms
- **Documentation**: Confluence or similar for knowledge management

## Budget-Conscious Laboratory Options

### Minimal Viable Lab Setup
- **Space**: Repurposed classroom or maker space (50+ sq meters)
- **Core Robot**: Single affordable humanoid or mobile platform
- **Computing**: Repurposed high-end desktops or laptops
- **Basic Tools**: Essential tools for assembly and debugging

### Hardware Budgets and Recommendations
- **Budget Range**: $10K-$50K for entry-level lab
- **Mid-Range**: $50K-$200K for comprehensive setup
- **Advanced Lab**: $200K+ for cutting-edge research capabilities

#### Cost-Effective Strategies
- **Open Source**: Utilize open-source software and hardware platforms
- **Partnerships**: Collaborate with industry for equipment loans/donations
- **Phased Implementation**: Add capabilities incrementally based on funding
- **Student Projects**: Leverage student-built platforms as lab assets

- **Emergency Stops**: Accessible emergency stops throughout the lab
- **Safety Barriers**: Physical barriers to contain robot operations
- **Personal Protective Equipment**: Safety glasses, closed-toe shoes required

- **Network Security**: Firewalls and intrusion detection systems
